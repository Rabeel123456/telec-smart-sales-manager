TELEC SMART SALES MANAGER - MULTI COMPANY LETTERHEAD UPDATE

1. Supabase SQL Editor mein supabase/ADD_MULTI_COMPANY_LETTERHEAD.sql run karein.
2. Is folder ki tamam files GitHub repository mein upload/replace karein.
3. Commit karein. Vercel deployment Ready hone ka wait karein.
4. Live ERP mein Admin/Super Admin login karein.
5. Settings > Companies & Letterheads mein teen companies nazar aayengi.
6. Har company ko Edit karein, scanned A4 letterhead JPG/PNG upload karein aur Save Company & Letterhead press karein.
7. Quotation, Delivery Challan ya Invoice banate waqt Company select karein.
8. PDF download karne par selected company ka full A4 letterhead background aur us par document data print hoga.

Recommended scan: A4 portrait, 2480 x 3508 pixels, JPG or PNG, clear and straight.
Top Blank Area field se document data ko letterhead header ke neeche move kar sakte hain.
